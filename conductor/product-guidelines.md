# Product Guidelines: IRON & GLOVES Boxing Club

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Tone:** Welcoming & Community-driven. The brand should feel professional and elite but remain inclusive and encouraging for beginners.
- **Prose Style:** Punchy & Minimalist. Use short, high-impact sentences and clear bullet points to mirror the speed and precision of boxing. Avoid fluff; get straight to the value.

## Visual Language
- **Imagery:** Abstract & Graphic. Focus on bold typography as imagery, stylized silhouettes, and high-texture close-ups of boxing gear. This approach avoids generic stock-template appearances and builds a high-fashion athletic aesthetic.
- **Color Usage:** Strictly adhere to the "Modern Stealth" palette (Matte Black, Slate Grey, Neon Volt). Use Neon Volt sparingly for high-impact calls-to-action and key interactive highlights.

## Motion & Interaction
- **Primary Pattern:** Scroll-Triggered Reveal Effects. Content should enter the viewport with high-velocity GSAP animations (e.g., quick slides, snappy scales) to maintain an athletic and energetic feel.
- **Micro-interactions:** Buttons and cards should have fast, reactive hover states to reinforce the sense of power and responsiveness.

## Accessibility Standards
- **Contrast:** Prioritize high contrast for all text. The Neon Volt and Pure White text must meet or exceed WCAG AA standards against the dark background.
- **Semantic HTML:** Ensure all high-impact sections (Hero, Programs, Pricing) use correct semantic tags for screen reader clarity.
- **Navigation:** Maintain a clear, simple navigation structure that remains intuitive even with heavy animation.
